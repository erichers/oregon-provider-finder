# NUCC Provider Taxonomy subset

Source file: `nucc_taxonomy_261.csv`

Downloaded 2026-09-27 from https://www.nucc.org/images/stories/CSV/nucc_taxonomy_261.csv

The American Medical Association, on behalf of the National Uniform Claim Committee (NUCC), grants a non-exclusive, royalty-free, non-transferable permission to use and distribute the Health Care Provider Taxonomy Code Set in products in the United States. The conditions that matter here: do not change the codes (format changes needed to include them in a product are allowed), do not create derivative works, keep the copyright notice and attribution, and do not imply that the AMA or the NUCC endorses the product.

`nucc-subset.csv` is a row and column subset of that file. It keeps the codes this app maps to a provider group. Code values, classification, specialization, and display name are copied unchanged. The AMA holds the copyright in the Provider Taxonomy on behalf of the NUCC.
